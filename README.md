## 👨‍🔬 Short Bio

Dr. Lijian Xu is an Associate Professor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. He received his Ph.D. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Chiba University in 2017, following his M.S. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and B.S. from Zhejiang University.

He has held research positions at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, and SenseTime, gaining extensive experience across both academia and industry.

His research focuses on **multimodal world models, intelligent robotics, and computer vision**, with particular emphasis on:

* **Efficiency** – Tackling visual token redundancy and computational bottlenecks via token compression, dynamic inference, distillation, quantization, and neural architecture search. Enables low-latency deployment in world models and pathology image analysis.

* **Generalization** – Integrating structured knowledge (e.g., physical priors) with data-driven learning through cross-modal prompt tuning and parameter‑efficient adaptation. Achieves robust performance under few‑shot and out‑of‑distribution scenarios.

* **Interpretability** – Enhancing model transparency via chain‑of‑thought reasoning and reinforcement learning, with uncertainty modeling and OOD adaptation. Facilitates explainable clinical report generation and reliable decision‑making.

He has published over **60 papers** in top-tier venues, including *Med (Cell Press)*, ICML, and *Pattern Recognition*. He is a core contributor to the **Puyi medical multimodal foundation model**, developed in collaboration with Shanghai AI Lab, and has participated in building one of China’s largest medical AI training infrastructures.

He has also worked closely with Huawei on **AI system migration from NVIDIA to Ascend platforms**, enabling full-stack domestic deployment. His translational work includes the medical AI product **SenseCare**, widely used in clinical environments.
