ABOUT ME

About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology (SUAT), China. I have previously worked with research teams at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, and SenseTime.

My research interests include multimodal learning, medical foundation models, computational pathology, efficient visual representation, and bio-inspired embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that combine data-driven learning with structured knowledge, physical principles, and reliable reasoning.

I have participated in research and engineering projects spanning medical AI, multimodal foundation models, and domestic AI computing platforms. I am interested in interdisciplinary research and collaborations with universities, hospitals, research institutes, and industry.

RESEARCH

Research Directions

Our research explores efficient, reliable, and interdisciplinary artificial intelligence.

01

Multimodal Artificial Intelligence

Multimodal representation learning, vision-language models, knowledge-guided learning, and efficient multimodal reasoning.

02

Medical AI and Computational Pathology

Medical foundation models, whole-slide image understanding, pathology image compression, and trustworthy clinical decision support.

03

Bio-inspired Embodied Intelligence

Bio-inspired perception and control, embodied agents, intelligent robotics, and learning from natural physical systems.

04

Efficient and Trustworthy AI

Efficient visual token processing, generalizable representation learning, uncertainty estimation, and trustworthy reasoning.
